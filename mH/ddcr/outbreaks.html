Active Outbreaks & Epidemiological Monitoring

The Department of Disease Control & Research (DDCR) tracks provincial and international infectious disease trends, issuing quarantine protocols and clinical guidance.

Status: Elevated Regional Alert

Seasonal Influenza (Type A - H1N1 / H3N2)

Regions: Northern & Maritime Provinces • Mandate: Polyclinic Flu Immunization

View Protocol →

Increased seasonal outpatient presentations. All citizens aged 6 months and older are advised to receive their quadrivalent influenza vaccination at local provincial health clinics.

Status: Continuous Genomic Tracking

COVID-19 (JN.1 / KP.2 Subvariants)

Regions: Nationwide Urban Centers • Mandate: Wastewater Surveillance & Paxlovid Access

View Protocol →

Wastewater surveillance indicates steady low-level viral activity. Provincial hospitals maintain free 5-day oral antiviral courses (Paxlovid) for high-risk citizens and seniors.

Status: International Clade I / IIb Watch

Mpox / Monkeypox (Clade I & Clade II)

Regions: Port Entry Screenings • Mandate: Rapid PCR & Ring Vaccination

View Protocol →

Active surveillance for international travelers arriving from regions with Clade I transmission. JYNNEOS ring-vaccination and tecovirimat stockpiles positioned at provincial centers.

Status: Agricultural & Wildlife Biosecurity

Avian Influenza (HPAI H5N1 Clade 2.3.4.4b)

Regions: Rural Agricultural Districts • Mandate: PPE & Dairy Pasteurized Standards

View Protocol →

Heightened biosecurity across provincial dairy herds and poultry farms. Testing of bulk milk and wastewater confirms zero mammalian spillovers into the human municipal food supply.
