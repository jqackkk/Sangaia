frequently asked questions sangaia health mandate
how do i apply for healthcare?

all citizens are automatically eligible for universal healthcare under the sangaian health mandate upon receipt of their sangaia id. you can present your physical or digital sangaia id at any provincial clinic or hospital for immediate registration.

what does the sangaian health charter cover?

the charter comprehensively covers preventative checkups, emergency room care, routine surgeries, organ transplants, mental health counseling, prescription medications on the national formulary, dental restorations, and corrective eye examinations at 100% state expense.

how do i find a doctor or specialist?

you may schedule an appointment directly at any public hospital in new jasper, new madison, liveland, sentinel, or columbia. referrals for specialized surgical disciplines are issued within 48 hours by your primary care physician.

what do i do in an emergency?

for life-threatening conditions, dial 9-1-1 immediately. for non-life-threatening medical guidance, you can call the 24/7 sovereign health nurse line at 1-800-sangaia-care.
