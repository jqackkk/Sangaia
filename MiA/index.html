Federal Interactions & Co-Governance Accord Constitutional Competence Charter

Governance in Sangaia is defined by clear constitutional boundaries: the federal branch manages defense, currency, and cross-border transport, while provinces retain complete authority over municipal policing, zoning, and primary clinics.

1. Exclusive Federal Domain
  • National defense & territorial airspace security (MdA)
  • Sovereign currency & constitutional debt ceiling (mT)
  • High-speed inter-provincial maglev tracks & canals
  • Foreign treaties & diplomatic embassies
2. Provincial Jurisdiction
  • Municipal police departments & civil peacekeepers
  • Primary & secondary public school administrations
  • Local zoning, building codes & commercial permits
  • Provincial parks & local road resurfacing
3. Shared Constitutional Powers
  • Environmental protection & clean water enforcement
  • Emergency healthcare hospital coordination (mH)
  • Highway highway patrol & weight-station licensing
  • Disaster emergency response & flood brigades
