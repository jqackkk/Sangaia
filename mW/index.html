Statutory Pay & Wage Standards (in Sangaian Dollars S$)

In accordance with the Sangaian Fair Labor & Economic Dignity Statute, all registered employers must compensate workers in official Sangaian Dollars (S$). Furthermore, under the Sangaian Constitution, the federal tax ceiling is legally capped at a maximum of 20%, ensuring workers retain the vast majority of their earnings.

Work Permit Applications & Visa Authorization

Non-citizen professionals and skilled tradespeople must hold an active Work Permit issued by the Ministry of Work before engaging in gainful employment within the United Provinces of Sangaia. Applications are reviewed under strict meritocratic standards.

Class A: Skilled Professional

For foreign professionals holding formal university degrees or accredited professional credentials.

Class B: Seasonal & Maritime

Valid for 6–12 months for agricultural harvests, canal maritime transit, and construction projects.

Class S: Priority Shortage Track

Expedited 5-day approval for candidates applying to Tier-1 critical need sectors.

Class D: Remote Digital Specialist

For global remote professionals resident in Sangaia providing services outside national borders.

Official Work Permit Application (Form WP-01)

Filing Fee: S$ 0.00 (Fully state-subsidized by the Sangaian Executive).

Other Work-Related Applications & Registrations

Access specialized regulatory forms, professional certification evaluations, workplace dispute arbitrations, and vocational retraining grants.

Form WP-02: Trade & Professional License Recognition

Foreign degree and trade license verification. Ensures credentials in medicine, engineering, electrical, or law satisfy Sangaian parity.

Form WP-03: Provincial Union & Labor Board Registry

Register a collective bargaining unit, certify a workplace safety council, or petition the Federal Labor Ombudsman.

Form WP-04: Workplace Grievance & Wage Claim

Confidential filing for unpaid overtime, unsafe working conditions, or employer breaches of the Sangaian Fair Labor Charter.

Form WP-05: Vocational Retraining & Apprentice Grant

State educational subsidy providing up to S$ 5,000 for workers transitioning into Tier-1 high-need technical or medical vocations.
