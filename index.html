sovereign weights & measures directorate official standards active

Sangaia standardizes upon sovereign, base-10 human-scaled measurements: панотерм (°пт) for temperature and мономер (мн) for distance. Both eliminate awkward imperial math while integrating cleanly with scientific metric conversions.

1. temperature standard • панотерм (°пт) °пт = 0.9 × °c = 0.5 × (°f - 32)

effortless mental conversions: from fahrenheit: drop 32, then cut in half (86°f → 54 → 27°пт). from celsius: subtract 10% (30°c - 3 = 27°пт).

• from fahrenheit (86°f): drop 32 (54), then cut in half → 27°пт.
• from celsius (30°c): subtract 10% (-3) → 27°пт.
2. distance system • мономер (мн) 1 monomer = exactly 2.5 cm

the mathematical sweet spot: defined as exactly 2.5 cm (98.4% identical to an inch), monomer scales cleanly into base-10 units while eliminating awkward imperial math.

• monomers to centimeters: multiply by 2.5 (10 мн × 2.5 = 25 cm).
• kilomers to miles: drop last three zeros and divide by 6 (60,000 мн → 60 ÷ 6 = 1.0 mile).
