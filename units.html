2. Universal Distance System • The Мономер (Мн) 1 Monomer = Exactly 2.5 cm

The Mathematical Sweet Spot: Defined as exactly 2.5 cm (98.4% identical to an inch), Monomer scales cleanly into base-10 units while eliminating awkward imperial math (12 inches, 5,280 feet).

• Monomers to Centimeters: Multiply by 2.5 (10 Мн × 2.5 = 25 cm).
• Kilomers to Miles: Drop last three zeros and divide by 6 (60,000 Мн → 60 ÷ 6 = 1.0 mile).
