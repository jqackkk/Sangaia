National Debt Ceiling & Fiscal Restraint Charter Healthy Solvency Ratio (18.7%)

Under the Sangaian Constitution, the United Provinces maintain a strict Zero Monetary Strings Attached doctrine. The federal debt limit is legally capped at S$ 45.00 Billion (and cannot exceed 25% of annual GDP). This permanent ceiling prevents deficit creep and ensures the state never passes bills to posterity marked as "insufficient funds".

1. Hard 20% Tax Cap

The government is constitutionally barred from taxing more than 20.0% of any citizen's earnings, requiring strict operational discipline.

2. Unanimous Ceiling Assent

Raising the debt ceiling requires unanimous assent from both the Aeretocrat and the Body of Electives, making debt inflation practically impossible.

3. 100% Tangible Asset Backing

All issued debt instruments must be collateralized by physical bullion reserves, sovereign maglev infrastructure, or maritime canal revenues.
