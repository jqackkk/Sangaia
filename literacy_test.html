Statutory Examination Standards • Article Six (⎰6.cp.cnsp) Passing Standard: 80% In All Categories

Pursuant to Article Six, Section ⎰6.cp.cnsp of the Constitution of the United Provinces of Sangaia, every applicant for citizenship must demonstrate competent knowledge across the foundational pillars of the Commonwealth. This examination consists of 40 questions across five constitutional categories.

Applicants must achieve a score of 80% or higher in all categories (at least 7 out of 8 correct per category) to be approved for citizenship and proceed to the solemn Swearing of the Oath of Allegiance.

Official Naturalization Examination Questions 0 of 40 Answered

Category I: The Flag of Sangaia, Insignia & National Creed

Questions 1–8 • Constitution Preamble & Sovereign Emblems

1. What do the primary colors of the Sangaian national flag represent?
2. How many stars are emblazoned upon the sovereign standard of Sangaia, and what do they signify?
3. What is the constitutional significance of the Central Cross and Starburst upon the national banner?
4. According to Article Zero (The Preamble), what fundamental principles guide the People of the United Provinces of Sangaia?
5. In the national coat of arms and civil insignias, what does the quill paired with the balance scale represent?
6. What is the foundational state motto and creed enshrined in the Preamble of the Constitution?
7. When displayed during official provincial commons and public assemblies, how must the Sangaian flag be flown?
8. How does the Sangaian flag reflect the relationship between the federal government and the provinces?

Category II: Sangaian History, Virtue & The Aeretocracy

Questions 9–16 • Article Four (The Structure and Framework of Government)

9. Under Article Four (⎰4.gs.ggs), what is the official constitutional form of government of the United Provinces of Sangaia?
10. How is the Aeretocrat elevated to office upon the demise or lawful removal of their predecessor (⎰4.gs.ggs & ⎰4.gs.dcdr)?
11. What anti-dynastic rule does Article Four (⎰4.gs.dcdr ℶ2) strictly ordain to prevent the usurpation of power by a single bloodline?
12. Who assumes temporary caretaker authority during an Interregnum upon the demise, sudden incapacitation, or removal of the Aeretocrat (⎰4.gs.sopatl ℶ1)?
13. By what formal constitutional mechanism may the Body of Electives impeach an Aeretocrat who abandons virtue or suffers cognitive decline (⎰4.gs.ipm)?
14. What tenure of office does the Aeretocrat hold under Article Four (⎰4.gs.dcdr)?
15. What is the constitutional maximum term for members of the Aeretocrat's Cabinet under Article Four (⎰4.gs.gcs)?
16. How does the Constitution differentiate an Aeretocracy from a dictatorship or hereditary monarchy (⎰4.gs.dcdr ℶ1)?

Category III: Fundamental Constitutional Rights & Civil Liberties

Questions 17–24 • Article One (Rights as a Citizen)

17. Under Article One (⎰1.rc.ghf), which six fundamental rights are declared inviolable for the natural life of every citizen?
18. What does Section ⎰1.rc.rtian guarantee regarding equality before the law?
19. Under Clause ℶ1 of Article One, what are the constitutional boundaries of religious freedom in Sangaia?
20. What constitutional protection does Clause ℶ3 establish regarding individual privacy and domestic communications?
21. What due process guarantees must be observed before any citizen can be deprived of life, liberty, or property (⎰1.rc.lpdp)?
22. What right does Section ⎰1.rc.gfth guarantee regarding peaceful assembly and civic association?
23. Under Section ⎰1.rc.isas, what specific legal requirements must be fulfilled before a search warrant can be issued?
24. According to Clause ℶ2 of Article One, what is the scope of private property rights in Sangaia?

Category IV: Structure of Government, Body of Electives & United Judiciary

Questions 25–32 • Articles Four & Five (Legislative & Judicial Powers)

25. Under Article Four (⎰4.gs.sggs), into what two principal branches or sectors is the Federal Government organized?
26. How many Representatives comprise the Body of Electives, and how often are legislative elections held (⎰4.gs.boe)?
27. How are seats distributed within the Body of Electives following a national legislative election (⎰4.gs.boe ℶ2)?
28. What supermajority thresholds are required to pass a new Federal Law under Article Four (⎰4.gs.lms)?
29. What extraordinary consensus is required to ratify an Amendment to the Constitution of Sangaia (⎰4.gs.lms ℶ1)?
30. How many Justices comprise the United Judiciary (Federal Judiciary), and how are they chosen (Article V, ⎰5.fpj.jca)?
31. What is the tenure of office for a Justice of the Federal Judiciary under Article Five (⎰5.fpj.jt)?
32. What unique constitutional status does Section ⎰5.fpj.eojc grant to the Federal Judiciary's power of Judicial Review?

Category V: Provincial Powers, Public Finance, Commerce, Education & Citizenship

Questions 33–40 • Articles II, III, VI, VII, VIII & IX

33. Under Article Three (⎰3.prl.dd), who serves as the chief executive officer of each Province, and how are they chosen?
34. What road safety violation penalties does the Constitution mandate under Article Three (⎰3.prl.pdmv)?
35. What is the strict constitutional ceiling upon outstanding national debt under Article Seven (⎰7.t.nd)?
36. What are the constitutional limitations on federal taxation under Article Seven (⎰7.t.tot)?
37. What are the legal characteristics and backing of the Sangaian Dollar ($) under Article Seven (⎰7.t.nc)?
38. What is the mandatory Mark of Record cypher for all legislative bills introduced under Article Eight (⎰8.clb.moc)?
39. Under Article Nine (⎰9.edu.psf & ⎰9.edu.rfc), what rules govern public education subventions and school revenues?
40. Under Article Six (⎰6.cp.cnsp to ⎰6.cp.roc), what are the fundamental qualifications and duties of a Sangaian citizen?

Statutory Examination Rules

  • Article 6 Standard: 80% passing threshold in ALL 5 categories.
  • Age Mandate: Minimum age of 13 years old.
  • Residence Mandate: 6 months minimum residence or employment.
  • Sole Allegiance: Dual nationality prohibited by law.
  • Civic Obligation: Voting in public elections is required by law.

Oath of Allegiance
