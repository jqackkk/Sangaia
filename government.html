Separation of Virtues • The Four Constitutional Branches Unalterable Law
1. The Executive Council & Aeretocrat:

Headed by the Aeretocrat as Supreme Executive Magistrate. Administers federal decree enforcement, foreign treaties, and whole-of-government synchronization.

2. The Council of Fiscal Rectitude:

Treasury magistrates with independent prosecutorial powers to block unconstitutional sovereign debt or predatory financial legislation.

3. The Federal Council of Governors:

Elected executive governors of New Jasper, New Madison, Liveland, Sentinel, and Columbia coordinating interstate commerce and transport.

4. The High Magistrates of Virtue:

Senior constitutional jurists reviewing civil rights, ensuring zero corruption, and adjudicating interprovincial legal accords.

The Five Sovereign Provinces Self-Governing Republics

Sangaia comprises five sovereign provinces, each with a regional assembly, distinct economic specializations, and full cultural liberties:

  • New Jasper (Shesk): Seat of federal government, sovereign technology, and legal jurisprudence.
  • New Madison (Madison City): Agricultural breadbasket, renewable biofuels, and educational institutes.
  • Liveland (Oaks): Maritime trade, oceanic fisheries, coastal tourism, and tidal energy.
  • Sentinel (Sentinel Ridge): Alpine mineral stewardship, geothermal energy, and forestry reserves.
  • Columbia (Columbia City): Cultural humanities, aerospace engineering, and fine arts.
