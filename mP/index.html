The Sangaian Broadcast & Truth Doctrine

Many people associate propaganda with censorship or brainwashing, but the Sangaian Ministry of Propaganda does the exact opposite. While it does permit what can be shown to the public on broadcasting systems such as electronic mail or television, it strictly refuses political or ideological bias. Its mandate is factual integrity, transparency of public assembly, and freedom from monopolistic commercial lobbying.

Under Article IX of the Charter, any public institution or citizen subjected to investigative allegations has a guaranteed right of unedited civic reply within 24 hours. Furthermore, all synthetic or artificially generated media transmissions must display a persistent visual verification watermark.

Reporter Qualifications & Press Accreditation

In Sangaia, the Ministry of Propaganda does not censor or disseminate state dogma. Its constitutional mission is the exact opposite: safeguarding public truth by enforcing rigorous journalistic verification, barring corporate and lobbyist pay-to-play, and certifying accredited press corps.

🛡️

1. Dual-Source Fact Verification

Every investigative claim requires a minimum of two independent, verified source documents prior to broadcast. Uncorroborated single-source claims are legally barred from terrestrial feeds.

🚫

2. Corporate & Lobbyist Firewall

Journalists are strictly prohibited from receiving undisclosed honoraria, corporate travel grants, or gifts from political lobbies. All media agency funding ledgers are audited by the Treasury.

⚖️

3. Guaranteed Right of Civic Reply

Any citizen or civil body named in an investigative story has an inviolable constitutional right to equal broadcast airtime for factual rebuttal within 24 hours without editorial manipulation.

🤖

4. AI & Synthetic Media Watermark

All synthetic audio, generative video, or digitally modified footage must bear a persistent high-contrast watermark and cryptographic provenance hash. Deepfakes carry strict felony penalties.
